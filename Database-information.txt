OPEN FOOD FACTS DATA IN GLUTENSCANNER
=====================================

GlutenScanner contains information from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org),
which is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL):
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

This document is published under ODbL section 4.6(b). It describes the METHOD
OF MAKING THE ALTERATIONS to the Open Food Facts database, so that anyone who
receives our product information can reproduce the differences between Open
Food Facts' database and ours.

The source data is Open Food Facts' own public dump. We do not redistribute
it - it can be downloaded directly from https://openfoodfacts.org/data


WHAT WE START FROM
------------------

The food.parquet export from Open Food Facts. The whole dump is imported;
no products are filtered out.

From each product we read only these fields:

    code, product_name, ingredients_text, brands, allergens, traces,
    labels, nutriments, images, countries

Everything else in the dump is discarded.


THE ALTERATIONS, STEP BY STEP
-----------------------------

1. CHOOSING ONE LANGUAGE PER TEXT FIELD

Open Food Facts stores names and ingredient lists as a list of {lang, text}
entries. We reduce each to a single string using a fixed priority, falling
back to the first non-empty entry if none of the preferred languages is
present.

    Product name      da, main, en, de, sv, nb, no, fi
    Ingredient list   da, de, en, sv, nb, no, fi, main

The Danish variants are additionally kept as their own fields
(product_name_da, ingredients_text_da) where present.


2. CLEANING THE TEXT

Applied to product names and ingredient lists, in this order:

    a. Remove NUL characters (0x00). This is the only character PostgreSQL
       cannot store in text/jsonb, and a small number of products in the
       world dump contain it.
    b. Strip HTML tags. Open Food Facts marks allergens inline, for example
       <span class="allergen">wheat</span>; we display plain text.
    c. Unescape HTML entities.
    d. Collapse all runs of whitespace to a single space, and trim.

brands, allergens, traces and labels have NUL characters removed but are
otherwise kept as they are.


3. REDUCING THE NUTRIMENT LIST

Only the per-100 g values for these ten nutriments are kept, each renamed
from <name> to <name>_100g:

    energy-kj, energy-kcal, energy, fat, saturated-fat, carbohydrates,
    sugars, fiber, proteins, salt

All other nutriments are discarded.


4. RECONSTRUCTING IMAGE ADDRESSES

Open Food Facts' dump gives an images list rather than URLs. We rebuild two
addresses per product and store them. The images themselves are not copied
and are served from Open Food Facts' own CDN.

FRONT PHOTO - the front* entry is chosen, preferring front_da, then front_en,
then the first available:

    https://images.openfoodfacts.org/images/products/<code>/<key>.<rev>.400.jpg

OCR TEXT - Open Food Facts runs OCR on uploaded photos and publishes the
result next to the image as <imgid>.json. We store that address so the
ingredient text can be fetched later:

    https://images.openfoodfacts.org/images/products/<code>/<imgid>.json

The photo is chosen among the entries whose key starts with "ingredients_"
and which carry an imgid, ranked by the language suffix in the order
da, de, en, sv, nb, no, fi; entries in any other language rank last. Note
that this address is built from the image's imgid, not from the key and
revision used for the front photo.

In both addresses <code> is the barcode split into groups of three digits for
barcodes longer than eight characters (3017620422003 becomes
301/762/042/2003), and used unchanged for shorter ones - the same layout
Open Food Facts uses.


5. MAPPING COUNTRY TAGS

Open Food Facts' country tags are mapped to two-letter codes and stored as an
array, used only for internal coverage statistics:

    en:denmark -> dk           en:germany -> de
    en:sweden -> se            en:norway -> no
    en:united-kingdom -> gb    en:france -> fr
    en:spain -> es             en:italy -> it
    en:switzerland -> ch       en:austria -> at
    en:netherlands -> nl       en:belgium -> be
    en:ireland -> ie           en:portugal -> pt
    en:poland -> pl            en:finland -> fi
    en:united-states -> us

Tags outside this list are dropped. Products are NOT filtered by country.


6. STORING THE RESULT

One row per barcode. The fields above are stored as a single JSON object,
alongside the barcode, the product name, the brand, the country array, and
two flags recording whether the product has an ingredient list and whether
it has an image.


ALTERATIONS MADE AFTER THE IMPORT
---------------------------------

MISSING INGREDIENT LISTS ARE FILLED IN FROM OPEN FOOD FACTS' LIVE API.
The dump is a snapshot, and products that were empty when it was taken often
have an ingredient list by the time we look. Products without one are looked
up individually against Open Food Facts' public product API, and the text is
written in when it exists. The same cleaning rules as above are applied.

MISSING INGREDIENT LISTS ARE ALSO FILLED IN FROM OPEN FOOD FACTS' OWN OCR.
Using the address stored in step 4, the published OCR result is fetched and
cleaned the same way.

No other changes are made to Open Food Facts data after import.


WHAT IS NOT OPEN FOOD FACTS DATA
--------------------------------

Our product table also contains records from other sources that are not
covered by this licence, and user-submitted corrections that are layered on
top of it when a product is looked up. Those records are not derived from
Open Food Facts and are not offered under ODbL.


ATTRIBUTION
-----------

Open Food Facts is credited inside the GlutenScanner app (Settings -> About)
and in the footer of glutenscanner.info, in every supported language, with a
