Privacy Policy for GlutenScanner

Last updated: 4 August 2026

In short

GlutenScanner has no user accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we collect no personal data. If you choose to submit a product, you upload photos of its packaging to us - anonymously and without personal information (see the section below).

Camera

The app uses the camera to read barcodes and ingredient lists (OCR) - both happen locally on your phone and are not sent anywhere - and for the optional product submission, where the photos you take are uploaded to us (see below).

Submitting products (optional)

If a product isn't found, or you want to correct it, you can choose to submit it. You take 3 photos (barcode, front, ingredient list), which are uploaded to our server so we can add the product to the database for the benefit of other users.

  • Anonymous: we don't ask for or store name, account or contact details. Location data (EXIF) is removed from the images.
  • Purpose: the images are used solely to create or correct the product in the product database.
  • Moderation: submissions are reviewed manually before they're added. Images that don't show a product are rejected.
  • Storage: if a submission is rejected, all images are deleted immediately. If it's approved, we keep only the front photo as the product image - the barcode and ingredient photos are deleted.

Deleting submitted images

Submissions are anonymous, but if you want a submitted image removed, email support@glutenscanner.info with the product name or barcode. We'll then find and delete the image.

Scan history

Your scan history (product name, barcode, gluten assessment and date) is stored only locally on your own phone. It's never sent to us or to third parties, and you can delete it in the app at any time.

Internet lookups

When you scan a product, the barcode (and in some cases the product name) is looked up - first in our own database at glutenscanner.info, and if the product isn't found there, with: Open Food Facts, Open Products Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Kassal.app, Salling Group, UPCitemdb and DuckDuckGo (web search). No information about you as a person is sent - only the product's barcode/name. The services' own privacy policies apply to their processing of the lookups.

Translation

Ingredient lists in other languages can be translated to Danish. The translation happens locally on your phone (Google ML Kit) and is not sent anywhere.

Data sources

Some of the product data comes from Open Food Facts and is used under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Permissions

  • Camera: barcodes, ingredient lists (OCR) and product submission
  • Internet: looking up product information and submissions
  • Vibration: confirmation when a barcode is read

Disclaimer

GlutenScanner is a helper tool, not medical advice. The assessments are based on databases and automated lookups, which may be incomplete or outdated. Always read the ingredient list on the packaging yourself, especially with coeliac disease or severe gluten intolerance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: support@glutenscanner.info
